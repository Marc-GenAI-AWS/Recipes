Challenge Description:
In dynamic security environments, frontline teams manage a constant flow of incident alerts - ranging from suspicious individuals and unattended items to access violations or sensor activations. While most turn out to be benign, each alert requires timely, consistent action. The challenge lies in maintaining high vigilance without overreacting - ensuring genuine risks are swiftly addressed, while false positives are resolved efficiently and turned into learning opportunities.
Solution Description:
Develop a Security Response Advisor - a fine-tuned AI model designed to assist officers in evaluating incidents, recommending proportionate next steps, and reinforcing best practices. The model should be trained on publicly available security rules and scenario-based examples, rather than any real or sensitive data. It should provide calm, context-aware guidance (e.g., “verify identity, confirm authorization, and document the event”), helping teams practice decision-making and incident assessment.
Outcome Description: The Security Response Advisor provides scenario-based guidance that helps standardize decisionmaking and strengthen operational procedures. It assists teams in interpreting general security principles, shaping or refining SOPs where needed, and aligning actions with JBS. Its recommendations are designed to complement, not replace, professional judgment - encouraging review and validation by SMEs. Over time, it promotes consistency, procedural compliance, and a culture of continuous learning across security operations.
