Challenge Description:
Bayer aims to position Nubeqa as the foundation of care in prostate cancer—helping men live longer and love the life they live. The challenge is to express that mission with consistency, clarity, and emotional depth across all audiences. Internally, teams need unified messaging that connects science with patient impact. Externally, the story must inspire trust among healthcare professionals, caregivers, patients, and policymakers. Participants must build a system that bridges medical data and human purpose—translating Nubeqa’s value into language that informs, engages, and moves people to act.
Solution Description:
Develop a Nubeqa Value Proposition Generator—a fine tuned open LLM (3B parameter class or smaller) trained on synthetic, brand aligned examples. The model should infer audience type from minimal context and adjust for tone, intent, and level of technical detail. It must translate Nubeqa’s clinical strength—efficacy, safety, and tolerability—into compelling, audience ready statements that remain scientifically accurate and emotionally authentic. The goal is to demonstrate how a smaller, targeted model can produce communication that strengthens Bayer’s internal alignment and enhances external engagement.
Outcome Description:
The resulting system transforms how Nubeqa’s story is told. For Bayer teams, it becomes a partner for training, message framing, and campaign creation—ensuring a consistent, purpose driven narrative. For external audiences, it delivers tailored, trusted messages that combine evidence and empathy, highlighting how Nubeqa gives men more time and more life to live. The outcome is smarter, scalable storytelling—uniting precision, emotion, and clarity around a shared vision of hope and care.
