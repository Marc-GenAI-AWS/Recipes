Challenge Description:
Axiom Bank is under increasing regulatory pressure to detect and prevent sophisticated money laundering while maintaining smooth customer service. Enterprise-scale AML models are too expensive to deploy broadly, while off-the-shelf AI assistants often miss subtle laundering typologies or over-flag benign activity, leading to compliance gaps or needless investigations.
Solution Description:
Host an AWS AI League event to develop a “Money Laundering Detection Assistant”—a custom fine-tuned model that supports frontline banking, compliance, and investigations staff in analyzing, explaining, and deciding action on suspected money laundering across business and consumer accounts. This solution will be built by fine-tuning a compact model with expert-level AML detection strategies, typology insights, and nuanced risk differentiation expertise. The assistant will recommend precise investigative steps, document legitimate cases for closure, and escalate only when appropriate—all while reducing the burden and cost of deploying massive language models.
Outcome Description:
The implementation will empower compliance teams with an affordable, role-specific decision support tool that delivers advanced AML detection, scenario-specific investigation workflows, and judicious risk flagging. This will result in higher detection rates of novel laundering patterns, fewer false positives, and more efficient operations—all while maintaining rigorous regulatory compliance, cost savings, and expert-level banking standards.
