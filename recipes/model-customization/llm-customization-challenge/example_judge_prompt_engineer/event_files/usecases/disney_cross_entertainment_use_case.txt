Challenge Description:
Disney offers a universe of magical experiences, from stunning parks and immersive resort stays to blockbuster streaming content and character-driven adventures. But with so many options, customers often miss out on the full spectrum of Disney magic across platforms and business lines. Generic recommendation engines can’t connect the dots—failing to recognize, for example, how a favorite Marvel series could inspire a theme park quest or how a recent trip influences the next streaming binge.
Solution Description:
The challenge: Build a “Disney Cross-Entertainment Roadmap Assistant”—a fine-tuned AI model that thoughtfully recommends new, enriching experiences tailored to what each customer has already enjoyed across parks, movies, TV, and more. Using only public information, the assistant will suggest next steps—streaming a related series, a hidden gem at the parks, upcoming events, seasonal tie-ins, or collectible merchandise—crafting multi-platform journeys that surprise and delight Disney fans of all ages and interests.
Outcome Description:
Disney gains a cross-business assistant that helps every guest, family, or superfan maximize their Disney experience—unlocking adventures at home, in the parks, and online. The result: higher guest satisfaction and engagement, increased lifetime value, and a seamless connection between digital and real-world magic, all enabled through safe, privacy-respecting AI.
