Challenge Description:
Wealth managers today face a new frontier: making financial advice both precise and personal. Clients often plan their futures using rough estimates, while advisors confront an overload of complex product data—fund prospectuses, structured product details, and performance models that can obscure clarity. The opportunity is to bridge those gaps through intelligent assistants that can simplify complexity, visualize real financial trajectories, and provide guidance that connects numbers to life goals. 
Solution Description:
Develop a Personal Wealth Guidance Assistant—a fine tuned LLM that bridges financial expertise and human understanding. The model should draw personalized insights from minimal client input, simplifying the complexity of HSBC’s products and services into clear, motivating guidance. It must illustrate trade offs, visualize outcomes, and coach clients toward smarter decisions that reflect their goals, lifestyle, and financial readiness. The aim is to show how smaller, purpose built models can deliver trusted, empathetic wealth conversations at scale.
Outcome Description:
The resulting system redefines how people experience financial guidance. Advisors gain a digital partner that makes tailored advice faster and more intuitive, while clients receive accessible, human sounding coaching that connects their life goals with the right HSBC solutions. The outcome is a modern wealth experience—transparent, collaborative, and confidence building—helping clients plan precisely, act purposefully, and stay in control of their financial journey.
