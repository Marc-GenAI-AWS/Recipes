Challenge Description:
Enterprise IT teams face mounting complexity as they integrate search, retrieval, and intelligent Q&A across vast policy and data silos. System architecture decisions have direct impacts on scalability, data governance, cost efficiency, and internal user experience. Large models provide powerful answers but are costly and difficult to secure in internal networks; traditional systems are cheap but rigid and unhelpful for complex queries.
Solution Description:
Develop an Internal Architecture Intelligence Assistant—a fine-tuned 3B model leveraging curated synthetic training data about systems integration, architecture trade-offs, and process optimization. The assistant should offer strategic design guidance, component selection advice, and stepwise deployment best practices for search, retrieval, security, and cost management in enterprise environments. The model must deliver actionable, context-aware responses that outperform generic 70B models in terms of relevance, detail, and practical architectural insight.
Outcome Description:
Architects and engineering managers use the assistant for advanced recommendations on system design, deployment, backup, recovery, and scalable automation. The model provides explainable trade-off analyses, cites specific technology stack benefits, and gives actionable implementation steps suited for internal enterprise contexts, empowering teams to build robust, agile infrastructure at lower operational cost.
