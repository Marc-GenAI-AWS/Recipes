Overall goal:
Validate the AWS architecture where we have our FSx data living in one availability zone can be used for a SageMaker Training Job where the instance lives in another availability zone in the same region. Our goal is to show that we don't have to replicate the data to the same availability zone where the SageMaker Training Job is run even though we'll need to transfer the data for the sagemaker training job.

Use Case:
Our data is 800TB and we don't want to pay to store is in multiple availability zones. However, the P5 instance types want to use for our model training are only available in a different availability zone. So we want to validate is we can use FSx NetApp ontap to mount the data to the SageMaker Training Job, allow the requested data to be moved over the network for the ephemeral training job
